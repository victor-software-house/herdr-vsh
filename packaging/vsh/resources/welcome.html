Herdr VSH

Herdr, the desktop app for terminals and coding agents, with terminals drawn like Ghostty:

  • ligatures and synthetic italics
  • kitty images, including the previews Claude Code draws
  • block-character images and mouse hover

It runs beside Herdr with its own home (~/.config/herdr-vsh) and its own terminals, so the two never share anything.

The installer puts Herdr VSH in Applications, replacing an earlier copy.
